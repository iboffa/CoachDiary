# Training Sessions — Position-Specific

## 1. Position-Specific Session Overview

Section summary: Position-specific training sessions isolate the skill demands and movement patterns unique to each position group — guards (PG/SG), wings (SF), and bigs (PF/C) — so that each player spends their development time on the skills most directly relevant to their role.

Position-specific sessions are run in parallel or sequentially with the rest of the team during individual development periods. Each session below is designed as a 60-to-75-minute standalone session. The format follows the five-phase framework: activation → skill focus → competitive application → conditioning → cool-down.

---

## 2. Guard Session (PG / SG)

Section summary: The guard session targets the skill set of the point guard (PG / 1) and shooting guard (SG / 2) — ball-handling under pressure, pick-and-roll reads, catch-and-shoot mechanics, and on-ball defensive positioning; guards are expected to handle the ball for 70-80% of each possession and must master both scoring and creating roles.

**Session duration:** 70 minutes
**Equipment:** 4 balls, 5 cones, 2 chairs

### Phase 1 — Activation (10 minutes)

- 3 minutes of ball-handling warm-up: stationary two-ball dribble (both hands simultaneously, then alternating; see Drill BH-01).
- 3 minutes of defensive footwork: lateral slide series — two metres left, two metres right, four metres left, four metres right, continuously.
- 4 minutes of dynamic stretching: hip circles, ankle rotations, thoracic rotation.

### Phase 2 — Skill Focus Block 1: Ball-Handling and Shot Creation (20 minutes)

- **Drill: Chair Dribble Series (BH-04)** — 15 minutes. Use both the elbow and wing positions. Focus on: attacking off the screen, change of pace before the move, and pulling up off the one dribble.
  - Rounds 1–2: Strong-hand attack (crossover at the chair, pull-up from the elbow north `{x: 190, y: 188}`).
  - Rounds 3–4: Weak-hand attack (opposite side).
  - Rounds 5–6: Reject move (chair as a fake; player attacks before using it).
- **Drill: One-Dribble Pull-Up Elbow Series (SH-04)** — 5 minutes. Eight reps per side (north elbow, south elbow). Focus on the gather balance and shooting pocket.

### Phase 3 — Skill Focus Block 2: P&R and DHO Reads (15 minutes)

- Two-man P&R reads with a big (a PF or C player, or a coach using a screen):
  - Rep 1–4: Drop coverage — big shows drop, guard reads and pulls up from the level of the screen.
  - Rep 5–8: Hedge coverage — big jumps out, guard navigates the hedge and attacks the recovering lane.
  - Rep 9–12: Switch — guard identifies the switch mismatch and attacks immediately.
- **Coaching focus:** The guard must verbalise the coverage they see before executing the counter. This builds the habit of reading before acting.

### Phase 4 — Competitive Application (15 minutes)

- **2-on-2 Pick-and-Roll game:** Guard uses a live big's screen and competes against two live defenders. Three-possession rounds. Score: one point per basket. Team with six points first wins. Two rotations.

### Phase 5 — Conditioning (5 minutes)

- Perimeter sprint circuit: Guard sprints from the north corner `{x: 87, y: 73}` to the south corner `{x: 87, y: 438}` along the baseline and across the half-court arc. Four repetitions, 10-second rest between reps.

### Phase 6 — Cool-Down (5 minutes)

- Standing hamstring stretch, hip flexor lunge stretch, shoulder cross-body stretch (30 seconds each). Team review: coach states one positive observation and one improvement point from the guard session.

## 3. Wing Session (SF)

Section summary: The wing session targets the skill set of the small forward (SF / 3) — versatile scoring on catches off cuts and screens, mid-range pull-ups, drive-and-kick reads, and the ability to defend both guard and forward positions; wings must be the most complete offensive and defensive players on most rosters.

**Session duration:** 70 minutes
**Equipment:** 4 balls, 4 cones

### Phase 1 — Activation (10 minutes)

- 2 minutes of light jogging around the court perimeter.
- 3 minutes of individual ball-handling: cone weave change of direction (Drill BH-02) — focus on all four moves (crossover, between-the-legs, behind-the-back, spin).
- 5 minutes of dynamic stretching: leg swings, hip circles, lateral bounds.

### Phase 2 — Skill Focus Block 1: Catch-and-Shoot off Movement (20 minutes)

- **Drill: Floppy Curl Series** — Wing cuts from the south corner using a pin-down screen (represented by a cone at the south block `{x: 145, y: 323}`) and catches at the south wing `{x: 330, y: 398}` for a catch-and-shoot three.
  - Rep 1–5: Curl cut (curl around the cone toward the elbow side). Catch-and-shoot.
  - Rep 6–10: Flare cut (flare away from the cone toward the corner). Catch-and-shoot.
  - Rep 11–15: Backdoor read (cone is 'cheating' — wing cuts straight to the basket). Coach lobs.
  - Three sets of 15.
- **Coaching focus:** Footwork on the catch — the wing must set their feet while the ball is in the air, not after catching it.

### Phase 3 — Skill Focus Block 2: Drive-and-Kick and Mid-Range (15 minutes)

- **Wing drive series:** Wing receives the ball on the south wing `{x: 330, y: 398}`, reads a passive closeout, and attacks in two directions:
  - Baseline drive: attack toward the south corner, finish or kick to the corner player `{x: 87, y: 438}`.
  - Middle drive: attack toward the paint, finish or kick to the top `{x: 370, y: 255}`.
  - Eight repetitions each direction. Two sets.
- **Mid-range pull-up:** After the drive, if the lane closes (coach signals "close"), wing stops at the mid-range area (approx. `{x: 235, y: 370}`) and pulls up for a jumper.

### Phase 4 — Competitive Application (15 minutes)

- **1-on-1 from the wing:** Wing receives the ball and plays 1-on-1 against a live defender. Wing may use any scoring option. Emphasis: wing must attack within two seconds of catching — no holding the ball.
- Three-possession rounds. Switch roles.

### Phase 5 — Conditioning (5 minutes)

- Wing sprint circuit: Sprint from the south corner `{x: 87, y: 438}` to the north corner `{x: 87, y: 73}` (baseline), then from the north corner to the south wing `{x: 330, y: 398}` (across the arc), then sprint back to the baseline. Four circuits, 15-second rest.

### Phase 6 — Cool-Down (5 minutes)

- Quad stretch, hamstring stretch, hip flexor stretch. Team review.

## 4. Big Session (PF / C)

Section summary: The big session targets the skill set of the power forward (PF / 4) and center (C / 5) — post footwork, drop-step and jump-hook finishing, pick-and-roll mechanics (roll and pop), setting legal and effective screens, and defensive positioning against post and ball-screen actions; bigs must be physical, technically sound, and able to make the correct roll-vs-pop read on every screen.

**Session duration:** 70 minutes
**Equipment:** 4 balls, 2 cones

### Phase 1 — Activation (10 minutes)

- 3 minutes of footwork warm-up without ball: pivot drills (front pivot, reverse pivot, jump stop) at the block. 10 reps each.
- 3 minutes of Mikan Drill (FI-01) at slow to moderate speed — focus on footwork, not speed.
- 4 minutes of dynamic stretching: hip circles, thoracic rotation, shoulder circles.

### Phase 2 — Skill Focus Block 1: Post Footwork and Finishing (20 minutes)

- **Drop-Step Series (SH-06):** 8 reps baseline drop step from the south block `{x: 145, y: 323}`, 8 reps middle drop step. Three sets.
- **Jump Hook from Both Sides (SH-07):** 6 reps right-hand hook (south block), 6 reps left-hand hook (north block `{x: 145, y: 188}`). Two sets.
- **Up-and-Under:** From the south block, receive the pass, shot-fake to draw the defender up, then step under and finish on the opposite side. 6 reps. Coach applies passive resistance.
- **Coaching focus:** Footwork must be automatic before speed is added. Bigs who look at their feet during post moves lose track of the defender.

### Phase 3 — Skill Focus Block 2: Screen Setting and P&R Reads (15 minutes)

- **Screen-setting technique:** Big sets ball screens on a stationary passer (simulating PG) at the top of the key. Coach evaluates: legal screen position (set before the guard arrives), correct width (shoulder to shoulder of a defender), and sealed hip-to-back position.
  - 10 screens with roll reads: after setting, roll hard to the basket (approx. `{x: 90, y: 255}`).
  - 10 screens with pop reads: after setting, pop to the three-point arc `{x: 330, y: 113}`. Receive pass and shoot a mid-range or three-point shot.
- **Roll vs. pop read:** Coach stands at the screener's defender position and signals which coverage: hands up = stay (roll); hands wide = hedge (pop). Big must read and execute the correct option.

### Phase 4 — Competitive Application (15 minutes)

- **2-on-2 Post Play:** Big receives an entry pass on the south block and plays 2-on-2 (with a wing partner) against two live defenders. Big may post up, pass out, or set a screen for the wing. Three-possession rounds.

### Phase 5 — Conditioning (5 minutes)

- Big conditioning circuit: Sprint baseline to baseline twice (two-length sprint), rest 20 seconds. Repeat four times. Targets the short burst anaerobic demands of post play and screen-setting.

### Phase 6 — Cool-Down (5 minutes)

- Hip flexor couch stretch (90 seconds each side), quad stretch, hamstring stretch (30 seconds each). Team review.
